# Home Operations

## Maintenance schedule
- **HVAC filter**: check monthly, replace every 1-3 months (1" filters ~90 days; sooner with pets/allergies/thick pleated). Match size printed on the old filter; arrow points toward the blower/duct (direction of airflow). Clogged filter = high energy bills, iced coil, dead compressor.
- **Water heater**: flush/drain sediment yearly (annually; twice for hard water). Test the T&P (temperature/pressure relief) valve yearly — it should discharge then reseat. Set thermostat 120 F (49 C): scald-safe + efficient. Anode rod check ~every 3-5 years.
- **Gutters**: clean twice a year (spring + fall), more under trees. Overflow rots fascia and floods foundations.
- **Smoke + CO detectors**: test monthly; replace batteries yearly (or when it chirps). Replace smoke alarms every 10 years, CO alarms every 5-7 (check date stamp on the back). CO alarms near sleeping areas + each level.
- **Dryer vent**: clean lint from the duct yearly — clogged vents are a leading house-fire cause.
- **Refrigerator coils**: vacuum every 6-12 months. **Range hood / dishwasher filter**: clean periodically.
- **Fire extinguisher**: check gauge (green zone) yearly; ABC-rated for home.
- **Reverse ceiling fans**: counterclockwise (down-draft) in summer, clockwise (up-draft, low) in winter.

## Electrical safety
- **Never work hot**: shut the breaker OFF and verify dead with a non-contact tester before touching wiring. Assume every wire is live.
- **Breaker keeps tripping**: it's doing its job — overload or short. Reset once; if it re-trips, stop and find the cause. Repeated tripping or a warm/buzzing panel = call an electrician, don't upsize the breaker.
- **Never replace a breaker with a higher amp rating** to stop tripping — the breaker protects the wire gauge (15A = 14 AWG, 20A = 12 AWG); oversizing = fire risk.
- **GFCI** (kitchens, baths, garage, outdoors, near water): test monthly with TEST/RESET buttons; trips on ground faults to prevent shock. **AFCI** protects against arc faults (bedrooms/living areas).
- Signs to call a pro: burning smell, scorched/discolored outlets, buzzing, frequent trips, aluminum wiring, ungrounded (two-prong) outlets, any panel work.
- Extension cords are temporary; never run under rugs or through walls. Don't daisy-chain power strips.

## Plumbing basics
- **Know your shutoffs**: main water shutoff (where the line enters, or at the meter) + individual stops under sinks/toilets and at the water heater. Test them before an emergency. Label them.
- **Water hammer** (banging pipes on valve close): failed/waterlogged air chambers or missing arrestors. Fix: install water-hammer arrestors; on old air chambers, drain the system to recharge them. Can indicate excessive water pressure — verify pressure is 40-60 psi (a pressure gauge on a hose bib checks it; >80 psi damages fixtures, needs a PRV).
- **Clogs**: plunger first; then a drain snake/auger. Avoid chemical drain cleaners (caustic, damage pipes/seals, dangerous if they don't clear). Never mix drain chemicals.
- **Running toilet**: usually a worn flapper or misadjusted fill valve — cheap DIY fix. **Dripping faucet**: worn washer/cartridge.
- **Winterize**: shut off + drain outdoor hose bibs before freeze; open cabinet doors and drip faucets during hard freezes to prevent burst pipes.
- Shut off water to the house if leaving for extended travel.

## Appliance care
- Clean the washing machine (monthly hot/vinegar or tub-clean cycle); leave the door ajar to prevent mold on front-loaders.
- Dishwasher: clean the filter, run a cleaner cycle; don't overload.
- Garbage disposal: run cold water during + after; no grease, bones, fibrous peels, or coffee grounds.
- Don't overload circuits with high-draw appliances (space heaters, microwaves) on one outlet.

## Seasonal prep
- **Fall**: furnace service, reverse fans, gutters, drain/blow-out sprinklers, disconnect hoses, seal gaps, chimney/flue inspection before first fire.
- **Spring**: AC service before first heat wave, gutters, check roof/flashing, exterior caulk, test sump pump (pour water in the pit — it should kick on).
- **Summer**: AC filter cadence up, check condensate drain line (clear the clog to stop overflow).
- **Winter**: pipe freeze protection, detector checks, keep heat >=55 F if away.

## DIY vs call-a-pro
- **DIY-friendly**: filter swaps, detector batteries, faucet washers/flappers, plunging clogs, caulking, gutter cleaning, thermostat swap (line-voltage excepted), outlet/switch replacement ONLY if comfortable and breaker verified off.
- **Call a pro (hard boundary)**: anything gas (lines, water heater/furnace burners, leaks), main electrical panel/service, adding circuits, structural (load-bearing walls, roof structure, foundation cracks), sewer main, major water leaks, mold remediation at scale, roof work at height.
- Permits: major electrical, plumbing, structural, and gas work usually require permits + inspection — skipping them creates insurance/resale problems.

## Safety boundaries -> Action
- **Smell gas (rotten egg / sulfur)**: leave now, don't flip switches or light anything, no phone inside; call the gas utility / 911 from outside. Do NOT DIY.
- **CO alarm sounds**: get everyone outside to fresh air, call 911/fire; don't re-enter until cleared. Common sources: furnace, water heater, generator, running car in garage. Never run a generator/grill indoors or in a garage.
- **Electrical shock/scorching/burning smell**: kill the breaker, call an electrician; don't touch water near live current.
- **Active water leak/burst pipe**: shut the main, then the power to affected areas if water is near outlets/panel.
- **Overhead power lines, panel interior, gas appliance internals, roof edges, structural members**: pro only, every time.

## Gotchas -> Fix
- **Filter arrow backwards**: reduced airflow, strained blower. Fix: arrow points toward the furnace/air handler (airflow direction).
- **Breaker upsized to stop tripping**: fire hazard. Fix: match breaker to wire gauge; find the overload/short.
- **T&P valve dripping**: not "just a leak" — over-pressure/over-temp. Fix: lower thermostat to 120 F, check pressure; if it persists, call a pro (risk of tank rupture).
- **Chemical drain cleaner didn't clear**: now caustic standing water over a plumber's hands. Fix: snake mechanically; disclose chemicals used.
- **No known main shutoff during a flood**: minutes of damage. Fix: locate + label the main today.
- **Detector chirping ignored**: dead unit in a fire. Fix: replace battery; if past the 10yr (smoke) / 5-7yr (CO) date, replace the unit.
- **Space heater on a power strip**: overload/fire. Fix: high-draw appliances into a wall outlet only.
- **Grease down the disposal**: hardens and clogs the line. Fix: grease to the trash, cold water while running.
