# K2T Quant Finance Rigor Contract

Purpose: improve quant, derivatives, portfolio, macro, market microstructure, and backtesting answers.

Required quant answer structure:
1. Define the instrument / strategy / metric.
2. List inputs.
3. State assumptions.
4. Provide formula or model.
5. Track units.
6. Perform calculation or validation.
7. Sanity-check magnitude and sign.
8. Discuss sensitivity.
9. Identify data-quality risks.
10. State limitations and next validation.

Derivatives / volatility expectations:
- distinguish price, payoff, delta, gamma, vega, theta, rho, implied volatility, realized volatility
- state Black-Scholes / binomial / Greeks assumptions if used
- explain skew, term structure, volatility surface, moneyness, expiry, liquidity
- include hedging and model risk caveats

Backtesting expectations:
- timestamp alignment
- no lookahead bias
- survivorship bias check
- slippage and fees
- liquidity / capacity
- corporate actions
- benchmark comparison
- walk-forward or out-of-sample validation
- drawdown and risk-adjusted metrics

Portfolio risk expectations:
- exposure, beta, concentration, correlation, volatility, VaR/CVaR, drawdown, stress scenarios
- distinguish marginal, component, and total risk
- include position sizing and liquidity constraints

Market microstructure expectations:
- spread, depth, queue priority, fill probability, adverse selection, latency, maker/taker fees
- distinguish paper fills from executable fills

Macro cross-asset expectations:
- rate, FX, equity, credit, commodity transmission channels
- regime dependency
- causality caveats
- data release timing
- scenario analysis

A 4.75+ quant answer must include assumptions, formula/model, validation, risk caveats, and data-quality limitations.
