# Product Management Answer Contract

Use for roadmaps, product plans, launch plans, stakeholder summaries, prioritization, and requirements.

## Required Sections

1. Objective
2. User or stakeholder problem
3. Scope
4. Non-scope
5. Requirements
6. Success metrics
7. Dependencies
8. Risks and mitigations
9. Rollout or launch plan
10. Decision log
11. Next actions

## Quality Bar

- Connect recommendations to measurable outcomes.
- Distinguish MVP from full solution.
- Identify owners or ownership gaps when relevant.
- Include readiness criteria before launch.
