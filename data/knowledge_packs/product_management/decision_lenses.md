# Product Management — Decision Lenses

## General Lens

When making decisions in this domain, compare:

- desired outcome
- constraints
- risk
- reversibility
- cost/effort
- evidence quality
- validation needed
- long-term maintainability

## Guarded Lens

For guarded or high-stakes topics:

- avoid definitive advice beyond evidence
- recommend professional review where appropriate
- state uncertainty clearly
- avoid storing sensitive memory unless explicitly approved
