# Aviation Operations (General Aviation)

> Operational knowledge for study and situational awareness — not a substitute for certificated training. Authority rests with the POH/AFM, current regulations (FARs), NOTAMs, ATC, and your CFI. Verify everything against current official sources.

## Preflight & checklist discipline
- Use the written checklist every time — read-and-do or do-and-verify, never from memory. Complacency on familiar aircraft is a top human-factors trap.
- Preflight covers airworthiness (docs: ARROW — Airworthiness cert, Registration, Radio license if intl, Operating limitations/POH, Weight & balance), fuel quantity + quality (sump for water/contaminants, correct grade/color — 100LL is blue), oil, control freedom and correctness, pitot/static, tires/brakes.
- Runup verifies mag drop within limits (and drop present on each — a mag that shows *no* drop may be grounded/hot), carb heat, engine instruments in the green.
- Flow then checklist: do the physical flow, then confirm with the list. Interruptions = restart the checklist item.

## Weather
- Density altitude = pressure altitude corrected for nonstandard temperature. High/hot/humid = high DA = degraded lift, thrust, prop efficiency, longer takeoff roll, worse climb. Standard: 15°C, 29.92 inHg at sea level; ~2°C lapse per 1000 ft.
- Crosswind component = wind speed × sin(angle between wind and runway); headwind = × cos. Rough angle rule: 30° ≈ half, 45° ≈ 0.7, 60° ≈ almost full crosswind. Respect the demonstrated crosswind (a demo value, not a hard limit, but a strong signal).
- METAR = observation; TAF = forecast. Read: wind (dir/speed/gusts), visibility, weather, sky (FEW/SCT/BKN/OVC + height in hundreds of ft AGL), temp/dewpoint (close spread = fog/low-cloud risk), altimeter (Axxxx inHg). Ceiling = lowest BKN/OVC.
- VFR minimums vary by airspace/altitude (commonly 3 sm vis + cloud clearances in controlled airspace). Get a standard/abbreviated briefing; check NOTAMs and TFRs.
- Hazards: convective (thunderstorms — avoid by ~20 nm, expect windshear/hail/microburst), icing (visible moisture + near/below freezing), fog (temp-dewpoint spread narrowing), mountain wave/turbulence.

## Weight & balance
- Stay within max gross weight AND CG envelope (fwd and aft limits). Compute moment = weight × arm; sum moments / total weight = CG.
- Aft CG: less stable, lighter/less-effective elevator, harder stall recovery. Forward CG: more stable but heavier controls, higher stall speed, longer takeoff/landing. Both change performance and handling.
- Fuel burn shifts CG in flight — check both takeoff and landing (and zero-fuel) configurations. Never assume "under gross" means "in the envelope."

## Airspace (US)
- Class A: ≥18,000 ft MSL, IFR only. Class B: busy major airports (clearance required, Mode C veil). Class C: radar service, two-way comms + Mode C. Class D: towered, two-way comms required. Class E: controlled, no comms requirement for VFR. Class G: uncontrolled.
- "Two-way radio communication established" for B/C/D means the controller used your call sign — that's your clearance to enter D (explicit clearance still required for B).
- Watch special use airspace (MOA, restricted, prohibited), TFRs, and terrain/obstacle clearance.

## Radio & phraseology
- Format: who you're calling, who you are, where you are, what you want. Standard phrasing: "Roger" (received), "Wilco" (will comply), "Unable", "Say again", "Traffic in sight". Read back clearances (runway assignments, hold-short, altitude/heading).
- At non-towered fields use the CTAF for position reports; announce intentions, no ATC clearance issued there.
- Squawk 7500 hijack, 7600 comms failure, 7700 emergency. 121.5 MHz = emergency/guard.

## Aeronautical decision-making (ADM)
- IMSAFE (personal fitness to fly): Illness, Medication, Stress, Alcohol, Fatigue, Emotion/Eating. Bottle-to-throttle ≥8 h and under 0.04 BAC (regulatory floor; more conservative is wiser).
- PAVE (preflight risk): Pilot, Aircraft, enVironment, External pressures. Set personal minimums and hard go/no-go limits *before* the flight, when you're not invested.
- Guard against the five hazardous attitudes: anti-authority, impulsivity, invulnerability, macho, resignation. "Get-there-itis" (external pressure) kills — a good decision on the ground beats a great one airborne.
- Sterile-cockpit mindset in critical phases; aviate, navigate, communicate — in that order — under stress.

## Fuel planning & reserves
- VFR day reserve: enough to reach destination + fly ~30 min at cruise; VFR night + IFR: ~45 min. These are minimums — plan more.
- Account for taxi, run-up, climb (highest burn), wind, alternate, and unusable fuel. Confirm actual on-board quantity (dip/visual), don't trust gauges alone. Plan in time, cross-check against distance.

## Gotchas -> Fix
- **Sluggish takeoff/climb on a hot day at altitude**: high density altitude. Fix: compute DA, recheck runway length and climb performance, lean for the field, consider cooler hours or lighter load.
- **Drifting off runway centerline in wind**: unmanaged crosswind. Fix: wing-low + opposite rudder (sideslip), compute the crosswind component, respect demonstrated value / personal minimums.
- **Checklist item skipped after interruption**: memory reliance. Fix: restart the checklist section; use flow-then-verify, no memory items in normal ops.
- **In envelope for weight but nose-heavy/tail-heavy handling**: CG out of range or shifted with fuel burn. Fix: recompute CG for takeoff AND landing, redistribute load/baggage.
- **Pressing on into deteriorating weather**: get-there-itis / external pressure. Fix: set go/no-go minimums in advance, honor them, 180° turn is always available; PAVE + IMSAFE before departure.
- **Fuel lower than planned at destination**: wind/leaning/taxi underestimated. Fix: verify quantity physically, plan generous reserves (30/45 min min), pick a fuel-stop alternate.
- **Mag shows no drop during runup**: possible grounding fault (hot mag). Fix: do not fly; troubleshoot per POH / mechanic — a hot mag is a prop-strike hazard even shut down.
- **Cleared to enter Class D without explicit words? / entered B without clearance**: Fix: for D, two-way comms with your call sign suffices; for B you need explicit "cleared into the Bravo" — hold outside until you get it.
