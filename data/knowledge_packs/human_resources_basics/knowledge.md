# Human Resources Basics (Educational)

> General educational reference. Not legal advice; employment law varies by jurisdiction. Consult HR professionals + legal counsel before acting on hiring, discipline, termination, or compliance.

## Hiring
- **Job description**: title, purpose, core responsibilities, required vs preferred qualifications, success metrics, comp range, location/remote; write for the outcome, not a wish list (over-specced reqs shrink + skew the pool).
- **Sourcing**: inbound applicants, referrals (high quality but can reduce diversity), recruiters, job boards, direct/passive outreach.
- **Structured interviews** — same questions, same order, defined rubric/scorecard for every candidate; predict performance far better than unstructured chats.
- Use **behavioral** ("tell me about a time…", scored via STAR) + **work samples / job simulations** (strongest validity) over gut-feel Q&A.
- **Reduce bias**: standardized rubrics, diverse + trained panels, structured debriefs (score independently *before* discussing), consistent process, remove non-predictive proxies; beware halo effect, similarity/affinity bias, and "culture fit" used as a bias cover (use "values add" instead).
- Define the scorecard + must-have competencies *before* interviewing; decide on evidence, not vibes.

## Onboarding
- Pre-boarding: paperwork, equipment, accounts, first-week schedule ready before day 1.
- Structure the first 30/60/90 days with clear goals, a buddy/mentor, role clarity, and manager check-ins.
- Cover: role expectations, team/stakeholders, tools + systems, norms/culture, and early wins.
- Strong onboarding measurably improves time-to-productivity + first-year retention; a bad first week predicts early attrition.

## Compensation + benefits (concepts)
- **Total rewards** = base pay + variable pay (bonus/commission/equity) + benefits + non-cash (development, flexibility, recognition).
- **Pay structure**: job leveling/bands, market benchmarking (percentile targeting), internal equity, geographic differentials.
- **Pay equity**: audit for unjustified gaps across gender/race for comparable work; document objective pay factors.
- Benefits (vary by jurisdiction): health/retirement, paid leave, statutory benefits; communicate total value, not just base salary.
- **Transparency + consistency** prevent perceptions of unfairness; ad-hoc pay decisions create compression + resentment.

## Performance management
- **Goal setting**: SMART goals or **OKRs** (objective + measurable key results); cascade from company -> team -> individual; align to role.
- **Feedback**: frequent + specific + behavioral; **SBI** (Situation-Behavior-Impact) model; separate real-time coaching from formal reviews; balance positive + developmental.
- **Reviews**: avoid recency + halo/horns + central-tendency + leniency bias; gather multi-source (360) input; base ratings on documented evidence across the whole period, not the last month.
- **PIP** (performance improvement plan): specific measurable gaps, clear expectations + support/resources, defined timeline + check-ins, documented outcomes; a genuine improvement tool, not just a paper trail for termination.
- Separate performance conversations from compensation conversations where possible so feedback isn't drowned out by pay reactions.

## Engagement + retention
- **Engagement** = discretionary effort + commitment; drivers: meaningful work, growth, autonomy, recognition, good manager, fair pay, belonging (the manager relationship is the top driver).
- Measure with engagement/pulse surveys + eNPS; act visibly on results (surveying without acting erodes trust).
- **Retention**: stay interviews (before people leave) + exit interviews (learn why they left); track **regretted attrition** separately from total turnover.
- Turnover is expensive (recruiting, ramp, lost knowledge — often a large fraction of salary); target retention of high performers specifically.

## Org design + roles
- Match structure to strategy: functional (deep expertise, siloed), divisional (product/geo focus), matrix (dual reporting, needs clear decision rights).
- Define roles with clear accountabilities; use **RACI** (Responsible/Accountable/Consulted/Informed) to remove ambiguity + overlap.
- **Span of control** (reports per manager) + layers affect speed + cost; too many layers slow decisions, too few overloads managers.
- Job architecture: leveling framework + career ladders (IC track + manager track) so growth doesn't require becoming a manager.

## Policies + handbook
- **Employee handbook** covers: at-will/employment status, code of conduct, anti-harassment/discrimination, leave, pay + hours, benefits, IT/acceptable use, confidentiality, complaint/grievance procedures, disciplinary process.
- Apply policies **consistently** — inconsistent enforcement creates discrimination + wrongful-termination exposure.
- Keep it current, acknowledged (signed receipt), and jurisdiction-appropriate; review with counsel.

## Compliance basics (general)
- Anti-discrimination + harassment (protected classes), wage/hour + overtime, worker classification (employee vs contractor), leave laws, workplace safety, data privacy of employee records.
- **Documentation is the backbone**: contemporaneous, factual, behavior-focused records of performance + incidents + decisions protect both employee + employer.
- Handle complaints via a defined process: intake, prompt + impartial investigation, action, no retaliation.
- Specifics (protected classes, thresholds, required postings/leaves) vary widely — **consult legal counsel**.

## Difficult conversations + termination
- Prepare: facts + specifics + documented history; address behavior/impact, not personality; be direct, respectful, and clear on the required change.
- **Termination**: no surprises (follow prior feedback/PIP), consistent with policy + past practice, documented business reason, dignity + privacy; involve HR/legal first.
- Cover logistics: final pay, benefits continuation, return of property, access revocation, references policy, severance/release if applicable.
- Progressive discipline (verbal -> written -> final -> termination) where appropriate; gross misconduct may warrant immediate action — verify with counsel.

## Pitfalls -> Fix
- **Hiring on gut / unstructured interviews** -> use structured interviews + scorecards + work samples; decide on evidence, score independently before debrief.
- **"Culture fit" as bias cover** -> replace with defined "values add" + competencies; diverse trained panels; blind non-predictive proxies.
- **No documentation** -> keep contemporaneous factual records of feedback, incidents, and decisions; undocumented issues can't be defended or fairly acted on.
- **Vague expectations** -> set SMART goals/OKRs + explicit role accountabilities (RACI); people can't meet unstated standards.
- **Surprise reviews / termination** -> give continuous specific feedback; formal review + termination should never introduce new information.
- **Annual-only feedback** -> shift to frequent SBI feedback + regular 1:1s; annual ratings alone are recency-biased + demotivating.
- **Inconsistent policy enforcement** -> apply rules uniformly + document exceptions; inconsistency = discrimination + wrongful-termination risk.
- **PIP as pure exit paperwork** -> make it genuine (support + realistic timeline) or you lose trust + legal defensibility.
- **Surveying engagement then ignoring it** -> commit to visible follow-up actions; inaction is worse than not asking.
- **Worker misclassification** -> apply legal control/economic-reality tests; misclassifying contractors invites back pay + penalties (see business_law_basics).
- **Pay decisions ad hoc** -> use bands + benchmarking + documented factors; audit for pay equity; inconsistency breeds compression + attrition.
- **Ignoring regretted attrition** -> track it separately, run stay interviews, and target retention of high performers.
- **Handling terminations/harassment alone** -> loop in HR + legal counsel early; process errors create serious liability.

## Learning + development
- Growth is a top retention + engagement driver; tie development to individual goals + role progression.
- **70-20-10** model: ~70% learning from stretch on-the-job experience, ~20% from coaching/mentoring, ~10% from formal training.
- Build career ladders + skills frameworks so expectations at each level are explicit; promote on demonstrated next-level performance, not tenure.
- Individual development plans (IDPs): current strengths, gaps vs goals, actions, timeline.

## Culture
- Culture = the behaviors actually rewarded, tolerated, and modeled — not the values poster on the wall.
- Leaders set culture by what they measure, reward, promote, and tolerate; incentives + promotions signal real values louder than statements.
- Psychological safety (safe to speak up, admit mistakes, dissent) predicts team performance + surfaces problems early.
- Reinforce desired behaviors through recognition, hiring/promotion criteria, and consistent leadership example.

## Manager effectiveness
- Managers drive engagement, performance, and retention more than any single perk; invest in manager training (feedback, 1:1s, delegation, difficult conversations).
- **Regular 1:1s** (recurring, employee-owned agenda) build trust + catch issues early; not status meetings.
- Common manager failure modes: avoiding hard feedback, micromanaging, unclear priorities, taking credit/assigning blame poorly — coach against these.

Employment law is jurisdiction-specific + changing. Use this as orientation; verify compliance-sensitive actions with qualified HR + legal professionals.
