# Macro & Cross-Asset Analysis
## Regime framing (growth × inflation)
- Four-box: growth↑/infl↓ = goldilocks (risk-on, equities+credit lead); growth↑/infl↑ = reflation (commodities, value, short duration); growth↓/infl↑ = stagflation (cash, commodities, real assets; bonds AND stocks can fall together); growth↓/infl↓ = deflation/recession (duration/Treasuries win, credit/equity hurt).
- Distinguish **level, change (Δ), and surprise** (vs consensus). Markets price the second derivative — a "bad" print above expectations can rally.
## Yield curve
- Shape: **2s10s** (10y − 2y) and **3m10y** slope. Steep = growth/inflation ahead; flat = late cycle; **inverted** (2s10s < 0, esp. 3m10y) = classic recession lead, historically 6–18m ahead. Curve **re-steepening from inversion (bull steepener via front-end rally)** often coincides with the downturn arriving.
- Decompose moves: real rate (TIPS) + breakeven inflation = nominal. Bear steepener (long-end selloff) vs bull steepener (front-end rally) mean opposite things.
## Credit spreads = risk gauge
- **IG (OAS)** and **HY spreads** widen as default risk/risk-aversion rises; HY−IG and CCC−BB tiering shows stress depth. Spreads leading equity weakness = warning; equities up while HY widens = divergence to respect.
- Watch pace: gradual drift vs gap-widening. Credit usually cracks before equities in a genuine cycle turn.
## USD / dollar liquidity
- **DXY** strength = global tightening; a strong USD pressures EM (dollar-denominated debt), commodities (priced in USD), and risk broadly. Watch **cross-currency basis** and reserves for funding stress.
- Fed balance sheet, reserves, RRP, TGA drive **net liquidity**; draining liquidity = headwind for duration-sensitive/risk assets.
## Commodities
- **Oil**: growth + geopolitics; backwardation = tight physical, contango = glut. Oil↑ feeds headline inflation → hawkish policy risk.
- **Gold**: inverse to real yields and USD; hedge for debasement/tail risk. **Copper** ("Dr. Copper") = industrial demand proxy; copper/gold ratio tracks rates/growth.
## Central bank reaction function
- Fed dual mandate (inflation + employment); ECB inflation-only; policy path priced via **fed funds futures / OIS / SOFR**. **Real policy rate** (funds − expected inflation) tells restrictive vs accommodative. "Higher for longer" vs pivot reprices duration and multiples.
- Forward guidance and QT/QE shift the curve more than a single hike surprise.
## Cross-asset correlations & risk-on/off
- **Risk-on**: equities↑, credit spreads↓, USD/JPY/CHF↓, cyclicals/EM/copper↑, VIX↓, yields↑. **Risk-off** = mirror; Treasuries/gold/USD/yen bid.
- **Stock-bond correlation is regime-dependent**: negative in disinflation (bonds hedge) → **positive when inflation drives both** (2022), breaking 60/40 diversification. Don't assume the hedge holds.
- Confirmation across assets strengthens a thesis: a growth-scare selloff should show credit widening, cyclicals lagging defensives, curve bull-flattening, and copper/gold falling *together* — if they diverge, question the narrative.
## Key data & their market read
- Growth: **PMIs/ISM** (>50 expansion; leading), GDP (lagging), retail sales, jobless claims (high-frequency turn signal). Labor: **NFP/unemployment**, wage growth (sticky-inflation input).
- Inflation: **CPI/core CPI, PCE/core PCE** (Fed's preferred), PPI, breakevens, 5y5y forward inflation (long-run expectations anchor). Base effects distort YoY — watch MoM annualized.
- Sequence a cycle: liquidity/policy → financial conditions → activity (PMIs) → earnings → labor (lagging). Front-run using the leading rungs.
## Volatility & positioning
- **VIX** = equity implied vol / fear gauge; **MOVE** = rate vol. Vol spikes mark risk-off and often local capitulation. Term structure: backwardation = acute stress, contango = calm.
- **Positioning/sentiment** (CFTC COT, fund flows, surveys): crowded one-sided positioning sets up sharp reversals on surprise — a contrarian input, not a timing tool alone.
- **Financial conditions indices** (spreads + USD + equity + rates) summarize the transmission of policy to the real economy; tightening FCI is the mechanism through which hikes bite.
## Equity plumbing: rates → multiples
- Equity value = earnings × multiple; the **discount rate** (Rf + ERP) drives the multiple. Rising real yields compress multiples, hitting **long-duration** assets hardest (high-growth/no-profit tech) while value/short-duration/cash-flow-now hold up.
- Earnings are the cyclical leg: in a growth scare, falling rates (tailwind to multiples) can be swamped by falling earnings (headwind). Decompose index moves into rate (multiple) vs earnings (growth) drivers.
## Business & policy cycle map
- Early cycle: rates low, curve steep, credit tightening→easing, cyclicals/small-cap/EM lead. Mid: growth solid, Fed neutral, broad participation. Late: curve flat/inverting, credit spreads at cycle tights (complacent), commodities/energy lead, defensives outperform. Recession: rates cut, curve bull-steepens, credit blows out, Treasuries/gold/quality win.
- Policy lag: monetary policy hits activity with a **long and variable lag (~6–18m)** — judge the *stance's* cumulative effect, not the last meeting.
## Gotchas -> Fix
- **Single-factor narrative** ("stocks fell because of X"): moves are multi-driver — check rates, credit, USD, positioning together for confirmation/contradiction.
- **Trading the level not the surprise**: assets price expectations — compare the print to consensus, not to "good/bad" in absolute terms.
- **Assuming bonds hedge equities**: fails in inflation regimes (positive stock-bond corr) — verify current correlation before sizing a hedge.
- **Nominal vs real confusion**: strip inflation for cross-time comparisons; a "record high" nominal may be a real decline.
- **Inversion = immediate crash**: the *lead* is long and variable; use the un-inversion/steepening + credit + labor rollover as the nearer trigger.
- **Correlation regime shift**: historical correlations break at turning points — the diversification you relied on disappears exactly when needed.
- **USD as afterthought**: dollar direction quietly drives EM, commodities, and multinational earnings — always check DXY alongside the thesis.
- **Ignoring liquidity plumbing**: net liquidity (QT, TGA, RRP) can override fundamentals for risk assets over months.
- **Trusting cycle-tight credit spreads**: the calmest spreads print right before the turn — pair spread *level* with the *rate of change* and the labor/curve signals.
- **Reading YoY inflation only**: base effects lag reality — MoM annualized and 3m/6m run-rates catch turns the YoY masks.
- **Confusing a hawkish pause with easing**: a stance stays restrictive while real rates are positive; the lagged effect keeps tightening conditions even after hikes stop.
