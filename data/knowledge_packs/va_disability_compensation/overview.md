# VA Disability Compensation — Overview

VA disability compensation is a monthly, tax-free benefit for veterans with a
**service-connected** disability. This pack covers how, once a condition is
service-connected, it is **rated by symptom severity into a percentage**, and how
multiple ratings combine. Source of record: **38 CFR Part 4** (the Schedule for
Rating Disabilities, "VASRD") on eCFR; general rules in 38 CFR Parts 3-4.

## The two questions (keep them separate)
1. **Service connection** (Part 3): is the disability linked to service? Requires
   (1) a current diagnosis, (2) an in-service event/injury/exposure, and (3) a
   medical **nexus** linking them. *This pack assumes connection is established.*
2. **Rating** (Part 4): how disabling is it? Each condition gets a **diagnostic
   code** and a percentage from its rating criteria.

## How a condition (ailment) maps to a percentage
- Each body system has a section (e.g., §4.130 mental, §4.71a musculoskeletal) with
  **diagnostic codes** for specific conditions.
- Each code lists **rating levels** (e.g., 0/10/30/50/70/100) with the **symptoms /
  severity / measurements** that define each level.
- The rating is the level whose criteria the documented evidence most nearly
  approximates (38 CFR §4.7, "higher of two where the disability picture more nearly
  approximates"; §4.3 reasonable doubt to the claimant).
- Ratings reflect **average impairment in earning capacity** (§4.1), not symptoms in
  isolation.

## Combining multiple ratings (it is NOT addition)
- Multiple disabilities are combined with the **Combined Ratings Table (§4.25)**,
  which works on *remaining efficiency*: each disability applies to what's left after
  the prior one (so 50% + 30% ≈ 65% → rounded to the nearest 10 = **70%**, not 80%).
- **Bilateral factor (§4.26):** disabilities of paired extremities (both arms, both
  legs) get an extra 10% of the combined value of those bilateral ratings added
  before combining with the rest.
- Final combined rating is rounded to the nearest 10%.

## TDIU (Total Disability based on Individual Unemployability) — §4.16
- Pays at the **100% rate** without a 100% schedular rating when service-connected
  disabilities prevent **substantially gainful employment**. Typical schedular
  threshold: one disability ≥60%, or a combined ≥70% with at least one ≥40%
  (extra-schedular referral otherwise).

## Other rating mechanics worth knowing
- **Pyramiding is prohibited (§4.14):** the same symptoms can't be rated twice under
  different codes.
- **Static vs. re-examined:** some ratings are subject to future exams; longstanding
  stable ratings can become protected.
- **Effective date** governs back pay; **0% (noncompensable)** ratings still
  establish service connection (useful if the condition later worsens).
