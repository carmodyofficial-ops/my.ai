# Safety Boundaries — VA Disability Compensation

This pack is **reference information, not advice or a decision.**

- **Not legal, medical, or claims advice.** It does not establish, guarantee, or
  predict any rating or award. Actual ratings are decided by VA adjudicators based
  on the evidence of record and C&P examinations.
- **Percentages are not a self-assessment tool.** A condition is rated on the
  documented symptoms/severity against the schedule criteria — not on a checklist a
  claimant fills in. Do not tell a user "you qualify for X%."
- **Cite the regulation.** Every rating criterion must be attributable to a specific
  38 CFR Part 4 section / diagnostic code. Never invent percentages, diagnostic
  codes, or criteria. If a value isn't sourced, say so.
- **The schedule changes.** 38 CFR Part 4 is amended over time; verify against the
  current eCFR before stating a specific criterion as current.
- **Service connection is separate from rating.** Compensation requires (1) a current
  diagnosis, (2) an in-service event/exposure, and (3) a medical nexus — this pack
  describes rating *once service-connected*, not whether connection is established.
- **Refer out.** For a real claim, point users to an accredited VSO/attorney/claims
  agent and VA.gov; do not act as their representative.
