# VA Disability Compensation — Monthly Payment Rates

> Source (authoritative): VA.gov — https://www.va.gov/disability/compensation-rates/veteran-rates/
> **Effective December 1, 2025.** Rates change annually with the COLA — re-verify
> against VA.gov before quoting. Amounts are monthly and **tax-free**.

Payment depends on the **combined** disability rating (see combined-ratings math in
`overview.md`) and dependents. 10% and 20% are flat (no dependent add-ons).

## Flat rates (no dependents factor)
| Rating | Monthly |
|---|---|
| 10% | $180.42 |
| 20% | $356.66 |

## 30%–100% by dependent status (most common cases)

| 40% | $795.84 | $882.84 | $43.00 |
| 50% | $1,132.90 | $1,241.90 | $54.00 |
| 60% | $1,435.02 | $1,566.02 | $65.00 |
| 70% | $1,808.45 | $1,961.45 | $76.00 |
| 80% | $2,102.15 | $2,277.15 | $87.00 |

## Other dependent categories (on VA.gov, not reproduced in full here)
For 30%+ there are additional amounts for: a dependent parent (one or two); a child
18–23 in a qualifying school program; and **Aid & Attendance (A&A)** for a spouse who
needs care. These add to the base amounts above. See the full VA.gov table for the
exact figures by rating.

## How to read it
- Find the row for the **combined** rating (not the sum of individual ratings).
- Pick the column matching the veteran's dependents; add the per-child amount for each
  child under 18, plus any school-age-child / parent / A&A add-ons.
- This is the **schedular** monthly amount; **TDIU** pays at the 100% rate.

**Reference only — not a benefits determination.** Actual payment is set by VA based on
the rating decision and verified dependents.
