# Health & Lifestyle

> General wellness information only — not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. No dosing, no drug-interaction guidance. Anything involving symptoms, diagnosis, medications, chronic conditions, pregnancy, or sharp/persistent changes: see a licensed clinician.

## Sleep hygiene
- Adults target ~7-9 h/night. Consistency beats duration alone: same wake time every day (including weekends) anchors circadian rhythm.
- Morning bright light within ~30-60 min of waking sets the clock; dim/warm light in the last 1-2 h before bed. Screens bright and close-up delay sleep onset.
- Caffeine half-life ~5-6 h — cut it ~8-10 h before bed. Alcohol fragments sleep and suppresses REM even if it speeds onset.
- Keep the bedroom cool (~18°C/65°F), dark, quiet. Bed = sleep only; if awake >20 min, get up and return when sleepy (stimulus control).
- Anchor: last big meal ~2-3 h before bed; taper fluids to limit wakeups.

## Exercise principles
- Progressive overload: gradually increase load, reps, or volume over weeks. Roughly ~10%/week ceiling on volume/mileage jumps to limit injury.
- Cardio: mostly Zone 2 (conversational pace, ~60-70% max HR) for aerobic base, plus a smaller dose of higher-intensity intervals for VO2 max. A common frame is ~80/20 easy/hard.
- Resistance: hit each major muscle group ~2x/week; ~10-20 hard sets per muscle per week is a productive range for hypertrophy. Train near-ish failure with good form.
- Recovery is where adaptation happens: 48 h before hammering the same muscle group hard; sleep and protein drive it. Deload periodically. Progressive soreness that doesn't recover = back off.
- Weekly floor from public-health guidance: ~150 min moderate (or ~75 min vigorous) aerobic + 2 strength sessions. Any movement beats none; sitting less matters independently.
- Warm up dynamically; build in mobility. Sharp joint pain (vs muscular fatigue) is a stop signal, not a push-through.

## Nutrition basics
- Energy balance drives weight: intake vs expenditure. A ~300-500 kcal/day deficit is a moderate, sustainable cut; surplus for gaining. No single food is magic.
- Protein: general active-adult range ~1.6-2.2 g/kg bodyweight/day supports muscle retention/growth; spread across meals. Prioritize when in a deficit.
- Fiber: aim ~25-38 g/day from whole plants — supports satiety, glycemic response, gut health. Most people under-eat it.
- Favor whole/minimally-processed foods, plenty of vegetables and fruit, adequate healthy fats (fatty fish, olive oil, nuts). Micronutrients come from variety, not mainly pills.
- Hydration: drink to thirst and pale-yellow urine; needs scale with heat and activity. Excessive plain water without electrolytes during long hard efforts can cause hyponatremia.
- Sustainability > perfection: a plan you can hold for years beats an optimal one you quit in three weeks.

## Stress, habits & mind
- Chronic stress and poor sleep amplify each other. Down-regulate with slow breathing (extend the exhale), daylight, movement, and social connection.
- Habit loop: cue -> routine -> reward. Build behaviors by making the cue obvious and the action small ("2-minute" starting version); habit-stack onto an existing routine.
- Environment design beats willpower: make good defaults easy (visible, frictionless) and bad ones hard. Track a simple streak; miss once, never twice.
- Behavior change is gradual — expect lapses; treat them as data, not failure.

## Ergonomics & daily movement
- Neutral posture at a desk: top of screen ~eye level, elbows ~90°, feet flat, wrists straight, lumbar supported. The best posture is the *next* one — change positions often.
- Break up sitting: stand/walk ~2-5 min every ~30-60 min. Set up light and airflow to reduce eye and mental fatigue.
- Screen eye strain: 20-20-20 — every 20 min, look ~20 ft away for ~20 s.
- Lift with hips and legs, load close to the body, spine neutral, no twisting under load. Build a movement "snack" habit — short walks after meals blunt post-meal glucose swings.

## Consistency & measurement
- Trends beat single readings: bodyweight, resting HR, and mood/energy fluctuate daily (hydration, sodium, sleep). Judge by weekly averages, not one number.
- Sleep, protein, steps, and strength sessions are the highest-leverage levers for most people — nail those before chasing supplements or niche protocols.
- Most single-nutrient supplements show little benefit in people who already eat a varied diet; food-first. Big claims, small evidence = skepticism.
- Sudden changes — unexplained weight loss/gain, new persistent fatigue, chest pain, breathlessness — are not "wellness" questions. Escalate to a clinician promptly.

## Gotchas -> Fix
- **Can't fall/stay asleep**: late caffeine, bright evening screens, irregular wake time, alcohol. Fix: fixed wake time, morning light, cut caffeine ~8-10 h prior, cool dark room, get out of bed if wired.
- **Progress stalls despite training**: under-recovering or under-eating protein. Fix: prioritize sleep, hit protein target, add a deload week, verify progressive overload is actually progressing.
- **Persistent soreness / nagging pain**: doing too much too soon. Fix: cap weekly increases (~10%), separate joint pain from muscle fatigue; sharp/persistent pain -> see a clinician.
- **"Healthy" diet, weight won't move**: liquid calories, portion drift, or unrealistic target. Fix: check total energy balance honestly, add protein and fiber for satiety, aim for a modest deficit you can sustain.
- **Afternoon energy crash / poor focus**: short sleep, dehydration, blood-sugar swings, no movement. Fix: protein+fiber meals, water, a short walk, protect sleep.
- **New habit doesn't stick**: relying on motivation, starting too big. Fix: shrink to a 2-minute version, attach to an existing cue, design the environment, never miss twice.
- **Symptoms, meds, or diagnosis questions**: out of scope here. Fix: consult a licensed clinician or pharmacist — this content is general wellness only, not individualized medical advice.
