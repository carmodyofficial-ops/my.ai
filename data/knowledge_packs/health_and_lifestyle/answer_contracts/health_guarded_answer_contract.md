# Health Guarded Answer Contract

Use for health, labs, symptoms, supplements, medication-adjacent, diet, exercise, and risk questions.

## Required Sections

1. Safety framing
2. What is known
3. What is uncertain
4. Practical low-risk steps
5. Red flags or escalation criteria
6. Questions to ask a clinician when appropriate
7. Avoided claims

## Quality Bar

- Do not diagnose.
- Do not claim certainty from incomplete information.
- Encourage clinician involvement for concerning symptoms, abnormal labs, medication interactions, or persistent issues.
- Separate general education from personalized medical advice.
