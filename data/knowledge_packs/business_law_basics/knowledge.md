# Business Law Basics (Educational)

> General educational overview only. Not legal advice; laws vary by jurisdiction and change. Consult a licensed attorney for your specific situation before acting.

## Business entities + liability
- **Sole proprietorship** — one owner, no separation; simplest + cheapest; **unlimited personal liability**; income taxed on owner's personal return.
- **General partnership** — 2+ owners; each partner personally liable (often jointly + severally) for partnership debts + others' acts; pass-through tax; use a written partnership agreement.
- **Limited partnership (LP) / LLP** — limited partners' liability capped to investment; general partner (LP) still exposed; LLP shields partners from each other's malpractice.
- **LLC** — members get **limited liability** (personal assets shielded) with flexible management + pass-through taxation (can elect corporate tax); popular for small business.
- **Corporation** — separate legal person; limited liability; **C-corp** taxed at entity level then dividends (double taxation), easier to raise VC + issue stock; **S-corp** = pass-through election with ownership restrictions.
- **Liability shield requires respecting the entity**: separate finances, adequate capitalization, formalities — otherwise a court may "pierce the corporate veil" and reach personal assets.

## Contracts
- **Formation** requires: **offer + acceptance** (mutual assent / "meeting of the minds") + **consideration** (bargained-for exchange of value) + **capacity** + **legal purpose**.
- Many contracts are enforceable orally, but the **Statute of Frauds** requires certain ones in writing (e.g., real estate, goods over a threshold, > 1-year performance) — jurisdiction-specific.
- **Key clauses**: parties + scope of work, price + payment terms, term + termination, **IP ownership/assignment**, confidentiality, warranties, **limitation of liability + indemnification**, dispute resolution (governing law, venue, arbitration), force majeure, assignment, entire-agreement (integration).
- **Breach**: material (goes to the heart -> excuses the other party + damages) vs minor. **Remedies**: compensatory damages (expectation), consequential (if foreseeable), liquidated damages (pre-agreed, must be a reasonable estimate not a penalty), specific performance (unique goods/property), rescission. Non-breaching party generally must **mitigate** damages.
- Get it in writing; a signed integrated contract controls over prior oral promises.

## Intellectual property
- **Copyright** — original works of authorship (code, text, art, music); arises automatically on fixation; protects expression not ideas; registration adds enforcement rights; long duration.
- **Trademark** — brand identifiers (names, logos, slogans) distinguishing source; rights build through use + registration; protects against consumer confusion; must be enforced or weakened; renewable indefinitely.
- **Patent** — novel, non-obvious, useful inventions; must file (examined); grants a time-limited exclusion; discloses the invention publicly.
- **Trade secret** — confidential business info with economic value from secrecy (formulas, algorithms, customer lists); protected only while kept secret via reasonable measures (NDAs, access controls); no expiry but no protection once leaked/independently discovered.
- **Licensing** — grant use without transferring ownership; define scope (exclusive/non-exclusive), territory, field of use, term, royalties, sublicensing.
- **Ownership default trap**: work by employees within scope is often "work made for hire" (employer owns), but **contractors usually retain IP absent a written assignment** — always get a signed IP assignment.

## Employment law basics (general)
- **At-will employment** (common in the US) — either party can end the relationship anytime for any lawful reason; limited by anti-discrimination, retaliation, contract, and public-policy exceptions.
- **Employee vs independent contractor** — classification turns on **control + economic reality** (behavioral control, financial control, relationship), not the label or a contract alone. Misclassification -> back taxes, wages, benefits, penalties. Tests vary (IRS, ABC test) by jurisdiction.
- **Anti-discrimination** — protected classes (race, sex, religion, national origin, age, disability, etc.) under laws like Title VII/ADA/ADEA (US); prohibits discrimination + harassment + retaliation.
- **Wage/hour** — minimum wage, overtime, exempt vs non-exempt classification (FLSA in US).
- **Restrictive covenants** — NDAs (confidentiality, broadly enforceable), non-solicit, **non-compete** (increasingly restricted/banned in some jurisdictions; enforceability varies widely). Draft narrowly (scope, geography, duration) or risk being void.

## Privacy + data protection (concepts)
- **GDPR** (EU) — applies to processing EU residents' personal data regardless of company location; principles: lawful basis (consent/contract/legitimate interest), purpose limitation, data minimization, transparency, individual rights (access, erasure/"right to be forgotten", portability), breach notification (~72h), significant fines.
- **CCPA/CPRA** (California) — rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale/sharing"; applies to businesses meeting thresholds.
- Common duties: privacy notice, lawful basis/consent, security safeguards, breach response, vendor/data-processing agreements, cross-border transfer rules.
- Collect only what you need; document why; honor deletion/opt-out requests.

## Liability, insurance, policies
- **Insurance types**: general liability, professional liability (E&O), product liability, cyber, D&O (directors/officers), workers' comp, employment practices liability.
- **Terms of Service** — governs use of your product: acceptable use, disclaimers, limitation of liability, IP, termination, governing law.
- **Privacy Policy** — required disclosure of what data you collect, why, how it's used/shared, and user rights; often legally mandated.
- Combine entity structure + contracts + insurance for layered risk management (no single layer is complete).

## Pitfalls -> Fix
- **Handshake deals / no written contract** -> put material terms in a signed writing with scope, payment, IP, termination, and dispute clauses.
- **IP assignment gaps with contractors/founders** -> require signed IP assignment + invention agreements before work starts; confirm work-for-hire won't auto-transfer contractor IP.
- **Worker misclassification** -> apply the control/economic-reality (or ABC) test, not the label; when unsure, consult counsel — penalties are steep.
- **Ignoring the liability shield's upkeep** -> keep entity + personal finances separate, capitalize adequately, follow formalities; commingling invites veil-piercing.
- **Copy-pasted or template contracts unread** -> read every clause (indemnity, liability caps, auto-renew, assignment); template gaps become disputes.
- **Overbroad non-competes** -> narrow scope/geo/duration or rely on NDAs + non-solicits; overbroad = unenforceable in many places.
- **Collecting data with no privacy basis/notice** -> publish a privacy policy, establish lawful basis/consent, honor deletion/opt-out; map data flows + vendors.
- **No limitation-of-liability / indemnification terms** -> cap exposure + allocate risk explicitly in contracts + ToS.
- **Using another's brand/content assuming "fair use"** -> clear trademarks + license copyrighted material; fair use is a narrow, fact-specific defense.
- **Letting a trademark go unpoliced** -> monitor + enforce; unenforced marks weaken or become generic.
- **Skipping insurance** -> carry appropriate coverage; contracts + entity shields don't stop every claim.

## Choosing + forming an entity (considerations)
- Weigh: **liability** exposure, **tax** treatment (pass-through vs corporate), fundraising plans (VCs typically want C-corps for stock/preferred), admin burden + cost, ownership flexibility, and continuity.
- Formation basics: register with the state/authority, adopt governing docs (operating agreement for LLC, bylaws + shareholder agreement for corp), get a tax ID, separate bank account, required licenses/permits.
- **Founder agreements**: vesting schedules, roles, equity splits, IP assignment, buy-sell/exit terms — settle early in writing to avoid disputes.

## Dispute resolution
- **Negotiation** (cheapest) -> **mediation** (neutral facilitator, non-binding) -> **arbitration** (private, binding, often faster/confidential) -> **litigation** (court, public, costly, slow).
- Contracts usually pre-select governing law + venue + arbitration; read these before signing — they dictate where + how you'd fight.
- Keep records + written communications; disputes turn on documented evidence, not recollection.

## Regulatory + risk awareness
- Industry-specific rules may apply (consumer protection, advertising/FTC, securities, sector licensing, export controls, accessibility).
- Compliance is cheaper than enforcement: fines, injunctions, and reputational damage exceed prevention cost.
- When entering a new market, product line, or hiring across borders, get jurisdiction-specific advice early.

Laws + thresholds are jurisdiction-specific and evolving. Treat this as orientation, not a substitute for an attorney reviewing your facts.
