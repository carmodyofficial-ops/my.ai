# VA Benefits & Claims — Overview

This pack covers VA benefits BEYOND the disability rating itself: how to file and appeal
a claim, and the major benefit programs — health care, education, home loan, pension,
and survivor/burial benefits. The companion **va_disability_compensation** pack covers
how a service-connected condition is rated into a percentage (38 CFR Part 4).

Source of record: **VA.gov** plus **38 U.S.C.** / **38 CFR**. All public-domain US
federal material. Eligibility and amounts change over time (annual COLA, PACT Act
expansions) — always verify the current rule at VA.gov. This is reference information,
not legal or claims advice; real claims should go through an accredited VSO/attorney.

See `knowledge.md` for the working reference and `safety_boundaries.md` for limits.
