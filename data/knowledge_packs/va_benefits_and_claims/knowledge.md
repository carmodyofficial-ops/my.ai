# VA Benefits & Claims — Working Reference

Authoritative sources: **VA.gov**, **38 U.S.C.** and **38 CFR** (public-domain US
federal material). Eligibility rules and dollar figures change — **verify current
details at VA.gov before relying on them.** Reference information, **not** legal/claims
advice; for a real claim, refer to an accredited **VSO/attorney/claims agent**. For how
service-connected conditions are *rated*, see the VA disability compensation pack.

## The claims process (disability)
1. **Intent to File (ITF)** — filing an ITF (VA Form 21-0966) sets a potential
   **effective date** and gives you **up to 1 year** to submit the full claim; back pay
   runs from the ITF date if granted.
2. **File the claim** — VA Form **21-526EZ** for disability compensation. The **Fully
   Developed Claim** path (submit all evidence up front) is usually faster.
3. **Evidence** — the three elements of service connection: a **current diagnosis**, an
   **in-service event/injury/exposure**, and a medical **nexus** linking them. Service
   treatment records, private/VA medical records, and **lay (buddy) statements** all
   count.
4. **C&P exam** — VA may schedule a Compensation & Pension exam (often a DBQ) to assess
   severity. Attend it; missing it can deny the claim.
5. **Rating decision** — VA assigns service connection + a percentage per 38 CFR Part 4.
   **Effective date** is generally the date of claim (or ITF date), or the date
   entitlement arose, whichever is later.

## Appeals — the AMA system (Appeals Modernization Act, since Feb 2019)
After a decision you have **1 year** to choose one of three lanes (choosing in time
preserves the effective date):
- **Supplemental Claim** (VA Form 20-0995) — submit **new and relevant** evidence;
  reviewed by a regional office.
- **Higher-Level Review** (VA Form 20-0996) — a senior reviewer re-decides **de novo**;
  **no new evidence**; an informal phone conference can flag errors.
- **Board Appeal / Notice of Disagreement to the BVA** (VA Form 10182) — three options:
  **Direct Review** (no new evidence/hearing), **Evidence Submission**, or **Hearing**
  before a Veterans Law Judge.
You can switch lanes within deadlines. (Pre-2019 "legacy" appeals follow the old path.)

## Health care
- **Enroll** with VA Form **10-10EZ** (online/phone/in person). Enrollment is sorted into
  **Priority Groups 1–8** (1 = highest priority, lowest/no copays). Service-connected
  veterans (esp. 50%+ or unemployable) land in the top groups.
- **PACT Act (2022)** expanded eligibility and **presumptive** toxic-exposure conditions
  (burn pits, expanded Agent Orange) — many post-9/11 and earlier veterans are now
  eligible; toxic-exposure screening is offered.
- **CHAMPVA** covers certain dependents/survivors of veterans who are permanently &
  totally service-connected or died from a service-connected condition.

## Education & training
- **Post-9/11 GI Bill (Chapter 33)** — up to **36 months**: tuition/fees (public in-state
  rate, or a capped amount at private/foreign schools), a monthly **housing allowance**
  (based on the school's location), and a books stipend. Benefit **tier scales with
  aggregate active-duty service** (e.g., 36+ months ≈ 100%). **Yellow Ribbon** covers
  overage at participating private/out-of-state schools. The **"Forever GI Bill"** removed
  the 15-year use-by limit for service after Jan 1, 2013.
- **Montgomery GI Bill (Chapter 30)** — older contributory program, flat monthly rate.
- **Transfer of entitlement (TEB)** — career servicemembers can transfer GI Bill months
  to a spouse/children (must be approved while serving, with a service commitment).
- **Veteran Readiness & Employment — VR&E (Chapter 31)** — for veterans with a
  service-connected disability and an employment handicap: training, education,
  counseling, and job placement, plus a subsistence allowance.
- **DEA / Chapter 35 (Survivors' & Dependents' Educational Assistance)** — education
  benefits for dependents of veterans who died from, or are permanently & totally
  disabled by, a service-connected condition.

## Home loan
- **VA-guaranteed home loan** — typically **no down payment**, **no PMI**, competitive
  rates, limited closing costs. Requires a **Certificate of Eligibility (COE)**.
- A one-time **funding fee** applies (varies by down payment and first vs. subsequent
  use) — **waived** for veterans receiving service-connected compensation (and Purple
  Heart recipients). Borrowers with **full entitlement** have no VA loan limit (since
  2020). Surviving spouses of certain veterans may also be eligible.

## Pension (need-based, wartime)
- **Veterans Pension** — for **wartime** veterans who are **65+** or **permanently &
  totally disabled**, with income/net worth **below the Maximum Annual Pension Rate
  (MAPR)**. **Aid & Attendance** or **Housebound** allowances raise the MAPR for those
  needing daily care or confined to home.
- **Survivors Pension** — a similar need-based benefit for a low-income surviving spouse
  or child of a wartime veteran.

## Survivors & burial
- **DIC (Dependency and Indemnity Compensation)** — tax-free monthly benefit to eligible
  survivors when a veteran dies from a service-connected condition (or was rated 100%
  P&T for the required period). A flat base rate, indexed annually.
- **Burial benefits** — burial in a **VA national cemetery** (gravesite, headstone/marker,
  perpetual care, military funeral honors at no cost), a **burial allowance** (higher for
  a service-connected death), a **burial flag**, and a **Presidential Memorial
  Certificate**.

## Boundaries
Reference only — eligibility and amounts are determined by VA on the evidence, not by a
self-checklist. Don't tell a user they "qualify"; never invent figures, form numbers, or
deadlines — if a value isn't sourced, say so and point to **VA.gov** and an accredited
**VSO**.
