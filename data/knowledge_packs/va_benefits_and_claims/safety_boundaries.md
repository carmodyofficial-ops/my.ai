# Safety Boundaries — VA Benefits & Claims

This pack is **reference information, not advice or a determination.**

- **Not legal, medical, or claims advice.** Eligibility and benefit amounts are decided
  by VA on the evidence of record — never tell a user they "qualify" for a benefit.
- **Cite the source.** Form numbers, deadlines, eligibility rules, and dollar figures
  must trace to VA.gov / 38 U.S.C. / 38 CFR. Never invent a form number, deadline, rate,
  or eligibility rule. If a value isn't sourced, say so.
- **Things change.** The AMA appeals system, PACT Act eligibility, GI Bill rates, the
  home-loan funding fee, and pension MAPR are amended over time — verify current values
  at VA.gov before stating them as fact.
- **Refer out.** For an actual claim or appeal, point users to an accredited
  VSO/attorney/claims agent and VA.gov; do not act as their representative.
