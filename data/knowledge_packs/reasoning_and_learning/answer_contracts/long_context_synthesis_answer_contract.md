# Long-Context Synthesis Answer Contract

Use when summarizing long histories, engineering turnovers, milestone chains, benchmark reports, or multi-file context.

## Required Sections

1. Source inventory
2. Timeline or milestone map
3. Current state
4. Completed work
5. Known defects
6. Important artifacts and paths
7. Open decisions
8. Recommended next steps
9. Risks
10. Validation checklist

## Quality Bar

- Account for every named milestone.
- Preserve exact paths, scores, and test identifiers.
- Do not omit the final item in a sequence.
- Call out uncertainty instead of inventing missing details.
